### 4 reasons efficiency matters for a big data business strategy
A successful business strategy is one capable of providing a solid foundation for processes to generate predictable and scalable results. Therefore, it is necessary to have cohesion between strategic planning and current business reality, in order to generate space for operational applicability.

In this sense, technology for efficiency in Big Data is essential, since it is a resource that offers a more precise and comprehensive business vision. In turn, decision making based on innovative knowledge improves the organization's results. Next, you will see the importance of driving Big Data efficiency in your business and the results that can be achieved.

#### Multiple aspects of data analysis
The concept of Big Data becomes evident when conventional systems no longer meet the demand for the volume of data generated throughout the workflow, in terms of handling and analysis of the large volume of information. In such a scenario, it is necessary to develop the analytical capacity to accommodate the new needs for interpretation and application of the information acquired on a daily basis. Thus, the term inserted in the universe of Information Technology differs from all other analysis formats, since it raises the interpretive level. Let's move on to the main characteristics that define it in practice.

#### Data Volume
Each company has a specific demand regarding the volume of data. If we assume a company in the telecommunications segment, we can undoubtedly imagine the very high flow of information to be stored, managed and translated into actions. In the midst of so much raw data, there is the information necessary to stimulate the company's income, but without the efficiency of big data it is impossible to identify this asset. In this way, technology enables the qualification of each useful information, with the aim of transforming it into practical actions and solid strategies.

#### Reduction of Operational Complexity
Generally, the data comes from different sources, which makes it difficult to define the correlation between the information, the crossing of the data itself. In this sense, efficiency in Big Data favors the hierarchy of the data structure, even if there are numerous sources of information. This provides operational efficiency and agility in decision making.

#### Greater Speed of Analysis
Big Data technology is one of the few that can keep up with the fluidity of today's data generation. In other words, it is possible to ensure the extraction of the meaning of each relevant information for operational processes.
