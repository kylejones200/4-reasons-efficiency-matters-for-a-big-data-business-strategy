In this sense, technology for efficiency in Big Data is essential, since it is a resource that offers a more precise and comprehensive business vision. Next, you will see the importance of driving Big Data efficiency in your business and the results that can be achieved.

In such a scenario, it is necessary to develop the analytical capacity to accommodate the new needs for interpretation and application of the information acquired on a daily basis.
